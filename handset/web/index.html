WELCOME TO PANE

Your iPhone, connected.

USB

Plug your unlocked iPhone into this laptop using a data cable. Tap Trust on the phone.

If no phone appears, install Apple Devices and try another cable or port.

  1. On iPhone, open Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode.
  2. Switch it on and restart the iPhone.
  3. Unlock it, confirm Turn On, and reconnect USB.

Keep your phone unlocked. Pane checks the result before installing its app.

  1. Open Pane on your iPhone. Trust your developer profile in Settings if iOS asks.
  2. Allow Bluetooth access and keep Pane open.
  3. Accept the Bluetooth pairing prompts on both devices.

The USB link is imported automatically. Wi-Fi is used for mirroring; Bluetooth is used for file transfers.

“Untrusted Developer” when opening Pane?

On your iPhone, open Settings → General → VPN & Device Management. Under Developer App, select the profile you used to install Pane, tap Trust and confirm. If iOS says Allow & Restart, follow that prompt and unlock after restarting.

Keep internet available for verification. Trust only the profile for the app you deliberately installed. If the signature has expired, renew it with Sign & install with Pane.

The “from developer” identity is controlled by iOS and the signing profile. Pane cannot hide or rename it. To avoid exposing a personal email, use an appropriate separate signing identity or Apple distribution option; check the identity Apple displays before sharing a build.

Your existing settings and paired phones are preserved by updates.

    GET STARTED

    iPhone setup

    Five steps. Keep your iPhone unlocked and your USB cable nearby.

    Connect your iPhone to begin.

    01 Connect and initiate setup

    Install Apple Devices for the USB driver. Plug in your iPhone, unlock it, tap Trust This Computer, then select it below. Mirroring requires iOS 27+; file sharing requires iOS 17+ and Bluetooth Low Energy.

    Pane asks the iPhone to reveal Developer Mode. If iOS refuses, install the companion in step 2, then retry.

    02 Install Pane

    Pane signs and installs its iPhone companion over USB. Apple signing is required; App Store review is not.

    “Untrusted Developer” when opening Pane?

    On your iPhone, open Settings → General → VPN & Device Management. Under Developer App, select the profile you used to install Pane, tap Trust and confirm. If iOS says Allow & Restart, follow that prompt and unlock after restarting.

    Keep internet available for verification. Trust only the profile for the app you deliberately installed. If the signature has expired, renew it with Sign & install with Pane.

    The “from developer” identity is controlled by iOS and the signing profile. Pane cannot hide or rename it. To avoid exposing a personal email, use an appropriate separate signing identity or Apple distribution option; check the identity Apple displays before sharing a build.

    Signing options & details

    Authentication uses Apple's services and the isideload anisette service at ani.iloader.app. Passwords are not saved. Signing certificates are protected by Windows. Free-account apps need renewal, normally every seven days; repeat this installation to renew.

    Already have a signed IPA? Install it directly below.

    Sign with AltStore Classic
    1. Download the unsigned Pane IPA from this fork's companion build artifacts.
    2. Follow AltStore's Windows guide for AltServer, its supported iTunes and iCloud versions, and Install AltStore → your iPhone.
    3. Authenticate your Apple Account directly in AltServer. Then open the IPA in AltStore on your iPhone to sign and install Pane.
    4. Trust your developer profile in Settings → General → VPN & Device Management if prompted. Free-account apps generally need refreshing every seven days.

    AltStore is an optional alternative. With Sign & install with Pane above, no separate AltStore installation is required.

    03 Enable Developer Mode
    1. Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode on the iPhone.
    2. Enable it and restart the iPhone.
    3. Unlock it, confirm Turn On, and reconnect USB.

    Pane checks both on the device. Managed-device restrictions may prevent this step.

    04 Bind USB and Wi-Fi

    Keep USB connected. Put both devices on the same trusted Wi-Fi network. This step pairs wireless mirroring and writes a private pairing file into Pane.

    On the iPhone, open Pane and tap Activate USB link. This loads the pairing from the trusted cable.

    05 Connect Bluetooth and transfer

    Allow Bluetooth in Pane. Keep it open and accept Bluetooth pairing prompts on both devices.

    Use Uploads to send files to your iPhone. Photos and files shared from Pane arrive in your laptop's Downloads folder. Bluetooth is slower than a cable, especially for videos; keep the companion in the foreground.

    Choose Cable or Wi-Fi above for mirroring. The volume buttons control the iPhone directly.

    Using Pane

    How to use Pane
    • Connect: choose Cable or paired Wi-Fi and press Connect. The same button disconnects.
    • Touch & type: click the picture to tap, drag to swipe, and scroll with the mouse wheel. Close this panel to type on the iPhone when Keyboard → iPhone is on.
    • Home & Siri: click the house to go Home. Hold it for a moment to invoke Siri (if enabled on your iPhone). Right-click the picture for Home.
    • Side buttons: adjust volume or open the app switcher. Click the bell for Notification Center or the sliders for Control Center. Each press performs the same gesture.
    • Phone size: the picture and frame follow the connected display’s live proportions, including rotation and display-size changes. The initial outline is a placeholder until the first video frame arrives.
    • Window: hover over the three lines for Minimize, with Maximize/Restore and Close above it. Drag the pane label in this panel to move the window. Drag a phone corner to resize; the crosshair keeps Pane on top.
    • Connection panel: the arrow opens and closes this overlay. Escape also closes it. Use Restart screen if the picture freezes.
