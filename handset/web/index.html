GET STARTED

iPhone setup

Five steps. Keep your iPhone unlocked and your USB cable nearby.

Connect your iPhone to begin.

01 Connect and initiate setup

Install Apple Devices for the USB driver. Plug in your iPhone, unlock it, tap Trust This Computer, then select it below. Mirroring requires iOS 27+; file sharing requires iOS 17+ and Bluetooth Low Energy.

Pane asks the iPhone to reveal Developer Mode. If iOS refuses, install the companion in step 2, then retry.

02 Install Pane

Pane signs and installs its iPhone companion over USB. Apple signing is required; App Store review is not.

Pane can sign and install its companion directly. Sign in with your Apple Account to install the included companion.

Authentication uses Apple's services and the isideload anisette service at ani.iloader.app. Passwords are not saved. Signing certificates are protected by Windows. Free-account apps need renewal, normally every seven days; repeat this installation to renew.

Already have a signed IPA? Install it directly below.

Sign with AltStore Classic
  1. Download the unsigned Pane IPA from this fork's companion build artifacts.
  2. Follow AltStore's Windows guide for AltServer, its supported iTunes and iCloud versions, and Install AltStore → your iPhone.
  3. Authenticate your Apple Account directly in AltServer. Then open the IPA in AltStore on your iPhone to sign and install Pane.
  4. Trust your developer profile in Settings → General → VPN & Device Management if prompted. Free-account apps generally need refreshing every seven days.

AltStore is an optional alternative. With Sign & install with Pane above, no separate AltStore installation is required.

03 Enable Developer Mode
  1. Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode on the iPhone.
  2. Enable it and restart the iPhone.
  3. Unlock it, confirm Turn On, and reconnect USB.

Pane checks both on the device. Managed-device restrictions may prevent this step.

04 Bind USB and Wi-Fi

Keep USB connected. Put both devices on the same trusted Wi-Fi network. This step pairs wireless mirroring and writes a private pairing file into Pane.

On the iPhone, open Pane and tap Activate USB link. This loads the pairing from the trusted cable.

05 Connect Bluetooth and transfer

Allow Bluetooth in Pane. Keep it open and accept Bluetooth pairing prompts on both devices.

Use Uploads to send files to your iPhone. Photos and files shared from Pane arrive in your laptop's Downloads folder. Bluetooth is slower than a cable, especially for videos; keep the companion in the foreground.

Choose Cable or Wi-Fi above for mirroring. The volume buttons control the iPhone directly.
