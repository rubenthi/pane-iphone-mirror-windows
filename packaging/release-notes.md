Pane 1.7.0 for Windows x64

- Connect identifies the mirrored iPhone over Wi-Fi or USB and opens Windows' Bluetooth media receiver. On first use, keep Settings → Bluetooth open on the phone and approve the matching pairing codes. A saved endpoint for a different phone is skipped when its hardware address conflicts.
- If the media connection cannot open immediately, Pane reports the Windows result and keeps the receiver available for selection on the iPhone. Disconnect and app exit release it. An open receiver is separate from audible playback.
- Disable the Windows system backdrop behind the shaped phone window. Keep the drag handle visible while the window buttons appear above it.
- Restore single-action Notification Center, Control Center and App Switcher buttons. Hold Home for Siri.
- Select a 15, 30 or 60 FPS presentation target. Pane skips redundant drawing and packet copies while reporting received and displayed frames separately. iOS controls the encoder resolution and compression.

To update, launch Pane, wait until the update shows as verified, close Pane normally and reopen. Pane.exe Properties → Details shows Product version 1.7.0.
